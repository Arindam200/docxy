# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome here, whatever your experience level, background, identity,
or first language. We want taking part in Docxy to be a harassment-free
experience for all, and we commit to behaving in ways that make the project open
and welcoming.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good faith. A question is not an attack.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person. Explain the reasoning behind it.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and say so when you change your mind.
- Respect that maintainers review in their own time, and that a change can be
  declined because of project scope rather than quality.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal and political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Discriminatory remarks about any protected or personal characteristic
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing a review
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies in every project space, including issues, pull requests, code
review, commit messages, and the repository itself. It also applies when you are
representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the
maintainer, [@Arindam200](https://github.com/Arindam200) on GitHub. Reports are
handled confidentially, and you will not be asked to confront the person
involved.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may warn, temporarily block, or
permanently ban anyone for behaviour that breaks this code. Maintainers who do
not follow or enforce it in good faith can be reported the same way.
